The present level of IMR (26 infant deaths per thousand live births, for the
year 2022) is less than one-fourth as compared to 1971 (129 infant deaths
per thousand live births). In the last ten years, IMR has witnessed a decline
of about 35%. The Figure-4 given below indicates that IMR at all India level
has declined from 40 to 26 in the last decade. The corresponding decline in
rural areas is 44 to 29, and for urban areas it is from 27 to 18, thereby
exhibiting about 34% and 33% decadal decline respectively.

Despite the decline in IMR over the last decades, one in every 38 infants
die within first year of their life at the National level (irrespective of rural-
urban).
