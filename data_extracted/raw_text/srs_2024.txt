The present level of IMR (24 infant deaths per thousand live births, for the
year 2024) is less than one-fifth as compared to 1971 (129 infant deaths per
thousand live births). In the last ten years, IMR has witnessed a decline of
about 38%. The Figure-4 given below indicates that IMR at all India level
has declined from 39 to 24 in the last decade. The corresponding decline in
rural areas is 43 to 27 (about 37% decline) and in urban areas from 26 to 17,
(about 35% decline). Despite the decline in IMR over the last decades, one
in every 42 infants die within first year of their life at the National level
(irrespective of rural- urban).
